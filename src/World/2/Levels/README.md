# World 2 Levels — Temple Sanctuary & Ancient Lands (`Level_0` – `Level_7`)

World 2 features **8 decluttered Temple modules** (`Level_0` through `Level_7`) covering the Temple Exploration Courtyard and all 7 unique Temple arenas (with natural Wilderness and Mountains relocated to `World/4/`):

- `Level_0`: `temple` (Temple Exploration Courtyard)
- `Level_1`: `temple` (The Ancient Relic Temple)
- `Level_2`: `temple_grand_fairy_temple` (Grand Fairy Temple)
- `Level_3`: `temple_rainbow_passage` (The Rainbow Passage)
- `Level_4`: `temple_sacred_sanctuary` (Sacred Sanctuary)
- `Level_5`: `temple_golden_chamber` (Golden Chamber)
- `Level_6`: `temple_jade_gallery` (Jade Gallery)
- `Level_7`: `temple_silent_shrine` (Silent Shrine)
