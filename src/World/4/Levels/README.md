# World 4 Levels — The Great Outdoors, Wilderness and Mountains (`Level_1` – `Level_40`)

World 4 features **40 assorted wilderness level modules** strictly bounded to **`Level_1` through `Level_40`** (explicitly **NO `Level_0`**, **NO `Level_N`/`Level_*`**).

All 40 levels are arranged in **8 assorted 5-biome rounds** (`[Desert, Forest, Plain, Mountains, Natural Cave] × 8`), with **zero fences, zero garden plants, zero triangular stone fences, and zero blocking rocks**, featuring elevated `"stone_platform"` structures to jump onto and ride over, authentic acoustic ambience, and terrain-matched footstep physics:

- **Round 1**: `Level_1` (`desert`), `Level_2` (`forest`), `Level_3` (`plain`), `Level_4` (`mountains`), `Level_5` (`wilderness_cave_limestone_grotto`)
- **Round 2**: `Level_6` (`desert_obsidian_erg`), `Level_7` (`forest_old_growth_redwood`), `Level_8` (`plain_tallgrass_prairie`), `Level_9` (`mountains_glacial_ice_ridge`), `Level_10` (`wilderness_cave_granite_rift`)

- **Round 4**: `Level_16` (`desert_red_sandstone_mesa`), `Level_17` (`forest_mossy_temperate_rainforest`), `Level_18` (`plain_golden_savannah`), `Level_19` (`mountains_alpine_snow_saddle`), `Level_20` (`wilderness_cave_lava_tube`)
- **Round 5**: `Level_21` (`desert_oasis_palm_wadi`), `Level_22` (`forest_autumn_maple_hollow`), `Level_23` (`plain_wildflower_meadow`), `Level_24` (`mountains_slate_switchback_pass`), `Level_25` (`wilderness_cave_marble_cathedral`)
