# Level 20 — Wheat Field (`wheat_field`)

**World 1 Position**: `Level 20` (First sub-arena of the Crop Fields block, seamlessly following `Level 19: Cattle Farm`).  
**Arena Module**: `src/Arena/Crop_Field/Wheat_Field/`  
**Path Surface**: Flat triangular **Slate** pavers (`1650 Hz` acoustic profile) cycling through 36 distinct colors (including **Caramel**), bordered on both sides by 12-inch (`0.3048 m`) Red, Gold, and Green triangular divider stones.

**Ambience**: Wind-swept golden wheat stalks rustling across open ground under an unaltered sky.
