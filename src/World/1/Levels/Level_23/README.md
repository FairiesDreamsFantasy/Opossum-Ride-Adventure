# Level 23 — Rice Field (`rice_field`)

**World 1 Position**: `Level 23` (Fourth sub-arena of the Crop Fields block).  
**Arena Module**: `src/Arena/Crop_Field/Rice_Field/`  
**Path Surface**: Flat triangular **Slate** pavers (`1650 Hz` acoustic profile) cycling through 36 distinct colors (including **Caramel**), bordered on both sides by 12-inch (`0.3048 m`) Red, Gold, and Green triangular divider stones.  
**Obstacles**: Stacked `"triangular_stone_fence"` (`1.4 m` height, `500 m – 2,000 m` spacing).  
**Wildlife & Ethical Farming**: Autonomous solar boom sprayers and terraced paddies paired with dignified human stewardship; feral pigs, unmounted moose, monkeys riding moose, and loose roaming monkeys.  
**Ambience**: Gentle rice shoots rustling beside terraced water reflections.
