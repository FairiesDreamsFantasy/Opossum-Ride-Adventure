# Juvenile Goat Kids (`src/Characters/Goats/Kids/`)

A **kid** is a juvenile goat. This folder defines the six juvenile caprine kids who accompany the 16 adult core cameos across the hillside pastures of *The Goat Farm* (`goat_farm`, World 1 Level 14):

1. **Pip** (Female, Snowy Cream, companion to **Snowy**)
2. **Clover** (Female, Tri-Patch, companion to **Patches**)
3. **Pebble** (Male, Slate Grey, companion to **Alpine**)
4. **Bramble** (Male, Warm Russet, companion to **Willow**)
5. **Sprout** (Female, Apricot Spotted, companion to **Freckles**)
6. **Sunny** (Male, Honey Gold, companion to **Bella**)

- **Standardized Biometrics**: `4.1 feet` length × `2.8 feet` height × `31 inches` chest × `19 inches` width (`size: "kid"`, `renderScale: 0.56`).
- **Voice & Ambience**: Uses soft high-register kid bleat synthesis (`405–470 Hz`, `GenericKidSynthesizer` harmonic curve) attenuated gently for pasture calm.
- **Herd Behavior**: Kids remain paired with their social herd clusters off-path, hopping playfully around grassy knolls and grazing peacefully near adult goats.
