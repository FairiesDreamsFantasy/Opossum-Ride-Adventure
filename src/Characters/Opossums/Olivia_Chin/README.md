## Biography & Lore
Olivia Chin is a dedicated female opossum of the Chin passel. Sister and sister-in-law to Jalissa Chin, Olivia is the only Black opossum in her immediate household. A former theatrical and circus performer who survived an extremist attack at a former fairground, she has become a passionate advocate for victims of violence and a vocal opponent of terrorism, exploitation, and Babylonian materialism. Living a clean, Babylon-Free Rastafari livity, Olivia brings courage, sharp observational instincts, and steadfast resilience to every journey.
