# Triangular Stone Fence Obstacle (`System/Building_Blocks/Triangular_Stone_Fence/`)

**Category:** Obstacle  
**Material:** Stacked Triangular Slate Stone Blocks  
**Primary Arena Assignment:** Crop Fields (`src/Arena/Crop_Field/` and all 6 sub-arenas)

---

## Overview

The **Triangular Stone Fence** is a modular physical obstacle constructed from 3 tiers of interlocking flat triangular slate stone blocks resembling a structured low field fence.

- **Jump Ergonomics:** Calibrated to a world height of `1.4` meters (matching the garden fence ergonomics, comfortably cleared by an opossum jump apex of `3.0` meters and clearance threshold `playerY > 0.8` meters).
- **Spacing Discipline:** Placed strictly between **500 meters (minimum)** and **2,000 meters (maximum)** apart along Crop Field courses, keeping the trail tactical, rhythmic, and enjoyable.
- **Acoustic Profile:** Resonates with **Slate** lithic acoustics (`1650 Hz` resonant center, `Q = 4.8`, `105 Hz` low-end thud).
- **Exclusion Rule:** In Crop Field arenas, this obstacle completely replaces rocks, garden plants, and wooden garden fences.
