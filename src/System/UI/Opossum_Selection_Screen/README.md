## Overview
The Opossum Selection Screen is the central navigation and mount selection hub of the Opossum Ride Adventure game. It bridges user interaction, accessibility screen-reader narration (`r` key for mount profiles, `Shift+R` for rider profiles), and precise mathematical model rendering across multiple categories.

## Architecture & Tabs
1. **Crafted ("Crafted By Fairies Dreams & Fantasy")**:
   - Houses exclusively the **19 sacred handcrafted female (Jill) opossums** (Melissa, Ashley, Amara Qin, Saffron Rose, Jalissa Chin, Arden-Rosie Kone-Reynolds, Jahmella Rose, Dagmar Kone-Reynolds, Agape Rose, Roxanne Kone-Reynolds, Tiana Qin, Wanda Opossum, Olivia Chin, Ruth Kone-Reynolds, Kady Rose, Sandra Opossum, Daisy Rose, Callie Rose, and Stacey Jasmin).
   - Protected against dilution or unauthorized mixing with male mounts.
2. **Compact ("Compact / Crafted")**:
   - Houses the **8 compact crafted Jack opossums** (Drake Kone-Reynolds, Rudy Kone-Reynolds, Eugene Rose, Arnold Rose, Melvin Qin, Safford Chin, Ivan Opossum, and Isaac Opossum).
   - Enforces the compact rider height upper bound of **4 feet and 4.44 inches (52.44 inches)**.
3. **AI-Generated / Generic**:
   - Dynamic procedural mounts and community selections with rate-limiting and token bucket shields.

## Accessibility & Hotkeys
- **`r` Key**: Triggers `NarrationRegistry.compileSelection` providing un-diluted biometrics (shoulder height, body width, body length, head width, head height excluding ears) before background lore.
- **`Shift+R` Key**: Triggers rider profile narration.
- **Grid Layout**: 8-column responsive canvas grid with arena-themed coordinate tracking.
