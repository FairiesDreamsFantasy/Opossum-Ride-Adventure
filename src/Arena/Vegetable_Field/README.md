## Architectural & Gameplay Rules
- **Path Surface**: Ground-oriented (`pathPattern: "ground"`) flat triangular **Slate** pavers (`1650 Hz` acoustic profile) cycling through all 36 colors (including **Caramel**, never `"Carmel"`), bordered on both sides by 12-inch (`0.3048 m`) Red, Gold, and Green triangular divider stones.
- **Obstacles**: Stacked `"triangular_stone_fence"` (`1.4 m` height, `500 m – 2,000 m` spacing). Zero rocks, zero garden plants, zero wooden fences.
- **Ethical Stewardship**: Autonomous solar produce tenders and precision irrigation booms paired with dignified human stewardship (zero slavery or plantation depictions).
- **Sky**: Completely unaltered.
