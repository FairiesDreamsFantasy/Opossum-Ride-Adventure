## 🔬 Scientific & Engineering Subsystems

### 1. Advanced Computational Physics (`System/Engine/Science/Physics/`)
* **Kinematic Motion Equations:** Precise analytical calculation of displacement, velocity, acceleration, and time increments.
* **Energy Mechanics:** Formally derived calculations for Gravitational Potential Energy ($E_p = mgh$), Kinetic Energy ($E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$), and Escape Velocity.
* **Vector & Matrix Mathematics:** Multi-dimensional vector operations and transformation matrices governing arena positioning and character movement.

### 2. Procedural DSP Audio Synthesizers (`System/Sound/Synthesizer/`)
* **FM Synthesis BGM:** Custom carrier and modulator oscillator routing with exponential ADSR envelopes and adjustable modulation indices for lush, procedural ambient soundscapes.
* **Biquad Resonant SFX:** Tunable lowpass biquad filtering with $Q$ resonance and dual-oscillator frequency detuning for precise, crisp acoustic chimes and jump swooshes.

### 3. Modular Registry & Directory Protection (`System/Registry/`, `Characters/`, `Arena/`)
* **Zero-Stub Policy:** No placeholder components or empty functions are permitted.
* **Folder-Level Guardians (`AGENTS.md`)**: Every directory and subfolder across the repository is guarded by security sentinels to preserve artistic and architectural integrity.
